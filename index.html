Topology details

A pole is a point where 3 or 5 edges meet instead of 4; loops can only turn, split or join there. View → Poles shows them.

Advanced (inflation physics)

Face, hands and feet

The face takes its shape from the Features layer: eyes, brows, nose base and mouth drawn in the front view, matched by height to the same features in the side view. Each finger and toe follows its own shape in the top view.

Anatomy coverage

Every quad is labelled with the body region it stands for, and every loop with the anatomical line it follows. The list checks the loops a character needs to bend and make expressions: ● there, ◐ partly, ○ missing.

Regions and density

Density is quads per area against the body's average: below 0.8× means few quads for the size.

Feature groups

Strokes of one colour on the Features layer form a group, marked on the surface facing their view. A group can make that area puff out or flatten, or pull the nearest ring onto its line.

Paint groups on the model

Skeleton

Fitted automatically when inflation finishes. Load rig takes another skeleton (JSON, BVH, or a glTF/GLB model's); joints are matched by name (Mixamo, Unreal, Rigify and similar).

Export

glTF carries mesh, skeleton and skin weights; OBJ keeps the quads, with the parts, groups and loops in a JSON file beside it.

Joints and body points

Which body point each joint sits on. Joints left without one keep their place between matched joints, or their offset from their parent.
