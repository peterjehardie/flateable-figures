Strokes are drawn thick by this amount before the inside is filled, so small breaks in an outline still close. If the tinted hull leaks into the background, raise it or draw the missing bit with the pen.

Arm cross-section

In a T-pose the side view sees each arm end-on. With Fill, click inside that shape in the side view and the arms take its depth, tapering with the front view.

Strokes

Outline strokes build the hull. Features (coloured) become tags. Height lines and notes are ignored by the maths. A cross-section in the side view (like the arm circle at the shoulder) sets the arm's depth.

Cage

Hands and feet

The round 8m-vertex wrist widens to the flat palm, and again at the knuckles, through 1-to-3 units: one edge becomes three, with a 3-edge and a 5-edge pole. Read the other way it is the usual 3-to-1 reduction from the fingers down to the wrist. Units never touch, so there are no 6-edge poles. Fingers are separated by web faces; the thumb leaves the side of the palm near the wrist. The foot leaves the front of the ankle and widens twice for the toes.

Face

Head rings sit at Loomis's face heights: mouth, nose base, eyes, brow. The loops are insets of face blocks. Eyes need 16 or more vertices round each ring.

Resolution

Layout

A pole is a vertex where 3 or 5 edges meet instead of 4. Loops can only turn, split or merge at poles, so pole placement is the topology. This cage puts them at the armpit and shoulder corners, at both ends of the crotch chain, and at the corners of the head, hand and foot caps. Shade by Poles to see them.

Subdivision splits every quad into four without moving the poles, so the flow of the cage is kept at every level.

Inflation

Pressure pushes every part outward at the same speed until it reaches its rounded profile, then lets go. The push is smoothed across neighbours so fine loops (eyes, mouth, finger webs) travel with their surroundings. Tension only smooths the surface. Hands and feet, built close to shape, get a gentler fit.

By default every cross-section inflates into a rounded profile that just fits the box the two drawings allow: it touches the front outline at its sides and the side outline front and back, but not the box's corners. 2 is an ellipse; higher values square it off; the far end lets the balloon fill the whole box. Hands and feet are left to the top view.

Fit to the drawings

Tag groups

Coloured strokes become tags on the surface that faces their view. Each group can change the balloon locally, or pull the nearest edge loop onto its open lines.

Paint on the model

Paint is stored as positions on the body, not vertex numbers, so it survives any change of topology.

Export

Lower levels take the cage's own vertices off the inflated surface, so a level-0 export is a low-poly retopology of the balloon with the same loops.
